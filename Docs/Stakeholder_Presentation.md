# Stakeholder Presentation

# Commercial Business Analysis of Airline Ticket Pricing


# Business Problem

Airline companies need to optimize pricing strategies while maximizing revenue and improving route performance.


# Project Objective

Analyze airline ticket pricing data to identify revenue opportunities, customer booking patterns, and high-performing routes.


# KPIs

- Total Flights
- Total Revenue
- Average Ticket Price
- Average Flight Duration
- Revenue by Airline
- Revenue by Route
- Revenue by Source City
- Revenue by Destination City
- Booking Window Analysis


# Key Findings

- Certain airlines contribute significantly more revenue than others.
- Ticket prices vary substantially based on booking window.
- Premium travel class generates higher average ticket prices.
- Some routes consistently outperform others in revenue.
- Source and destination cities contribute unevenly to overall revenue.


# Business Recommendations

- Prioritize investment in high-revenue routes.
- Introduce dynamic pricing based on booking window.
- Optimize schedules for high-demand departure times.
- Increase marketing efforts in high-value source cities.
- Monitor low-performing routes for optimization.


# Expected Business Impact

- Improved pricing strategy
- Better revenue optimization
- Enhanced route planning
- Stronger commercial decision-making
- Increased operational efficiency
