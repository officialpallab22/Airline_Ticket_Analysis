# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

# Project Title
Commercial Airline Performance & Pricing Analysis

# Business Problem

Airline management wants to analyze ticket pricing, customer booking behavior, route performance, and airline performance to improve commercial decisions and maximize revenue.

# Business Goal

Develop an analytical solution that identifies revenue opportunities, pricing patterns, customer booking trends, and route performance to support business growth.

# Stakeholders

- CEO
- Commercial Team
- Revenue Management Team
- Network Planning Team
- Marketing Team
- Business Analysts

# Success Criteria

- Identify high-revenue routes
- Identify pricing trends
- Identify customer booking patterns
- Build an executive dashboard
- Provide actionable business recommendations
