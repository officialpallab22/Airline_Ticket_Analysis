# Assumptions & Limitations

# Assumptions

- The dataset is assumed to be accurate and complete.
- Ticket prices are assumed to be in Indian Rupees (INR).
- Flight duration is measured in hours.
- Duplicate records have been removed before analysis.
- Revenue calculations are based on the ticket prices available in the dataset.

# Limitations

- Passenger count is not available.
- Airline operating cost is unavailable.
- Aircraft type information is unavailable.
- Profit cannot be calculated because operating expenses are not included.
- Actual booking date is unavailable.
- Cancellation information is unavailable.
- Delay information is unavailable.
- Seasonal demand factors are unavailable.
- Weather impact is unavailable.
